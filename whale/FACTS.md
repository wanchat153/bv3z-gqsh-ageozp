# The Exploding Whale: facts used

| Fact | Used as | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A dead sperm whale washed up south of Florence, Oregon; it was blown up on 12 November 1970 at 3:45 pm | caption 1, caption 8 | Amusing Planet; Mental Floss |
| About 45 ft (≈14 m) long, about 8 tons | caption 2 | Amusing Planet; Mental Floss |
| The Oregon State Highway Division was responsible, because Oregon's beaches were treated as highways | caption 3 | Amusing Planet; Mental Floss |
| They handled it like a boulder: break it up with explosives | caption 4 | Mental Floss |
| 20 cases of dynamite, about half a ton | caption 5 | Mental Floss; Amusing Planet |
| Scavengers (gulls) were expected to eat the small remains; the blast scared them off | captions 6 and 14 | Mental Floss |
| Spectators were moved back about a quarter mile (≈400 m) | caption 7 | Amusing Planet; Mental Floss |
| A column of sand and blubber about 100 ft (≈30 m) high | caption 9 | Amusing Planet |
| Blubber rained on the spectators; no one was hurt | caption 11 | Mental Floss |
| A brand-new car in the parking lot was crushed by a piece of blubber of about 300 lb (≈136 kg); the roof was bent down to the seats | caption 12 | Jalopnik |
| The state paid the owner the car's full value | caption 13 | Jalopnik; Mental Floss |
| Most of the whale stayed on the beach; it was moved with a bulldozer and buried in the sand | captions 14 and 15 | Amusing Planet; Mental Floss |

Characters are original stand-ins (the engineer, the workers, the car owner, the crowd). No real person is named or drawn.
The car's make is not shown (sources disagree), and it carries no brand or badge.
